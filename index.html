The scale

The score is how badly a video breaks the promise its title makes. Honest entertainment scores low — only broken promises raise the number.

ScoreVerdict
0–20LEGITdoes what the title says
21–40MILDdelivers, with some rambling
41–60MEHreal content buried in padding
61–80BAITpromises a lot more than it delivers
81–100SLOPfiller stretched out for watch time

How it works

  1. Your browser pulls each video's transcript from YouTube's own player API.
  2. It's scored by the AI provider you choose — Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or a local ollama — with your own API key.
  3. The verdict and a one-line summary render on the tile. Scores are cached 30 days.

No server. Nothing is collected. The transcript goes from your browser to your provider and nowhere else — against a local model it never leaves your machine at all. Open source, MIT licensed.
